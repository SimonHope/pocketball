The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email, age, location or any other personal information, and you can't type messages or names in the game.
  • Your progress is linked to a random code stored in your browser, not to who you are.
  • Anonymous gameplay stats are only collected if you say yes, and you can change that any time in Settings.
  • You can delete everything with Settings → Delete my data.

What we store

  • Game progress (on our server): your generated player name (for example "Zippy Hammy"), level, XP, coins, unlocked cosmetics, best times and daily-challenge progress.
  • Your game account: a random access code and a recovery code. Both stay in your browser; our server stores only one-way scrambled copies (hashes) of them.
  • Game settings (only in your browser): volume, camera and graphics choices.
  • Anonymous stats (only with your OK): things like "a course was started or finished", session length and graphics quality, tied to a random ID in your browser. We use them to find courses that are too hard and to fix problems.
  • Error reports: when the game crashes, the error message and page, with no ID attached.
  • Purchases (on our own website only): payments are handled by Stripe. We never see card details. We keep a record of which pack was bought and Stripe's reference, as tax law requires.

Our server sees your IP address while you're connected (the internet needs it to send you data) and uses it briefly to prevent abuse. It isn't stored with your progress or stats.

Children

Pocket Ball is designed so that it doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the age set by your country). There's no chat, no free-text names, no social features and no ad tracking by us.
